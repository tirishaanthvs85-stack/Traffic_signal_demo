1. The Problem with Static Fixed-Timers

Traditional traffic signals operate on fixed, predetermined cycles (e.g. 30 seconds green per lane regardless of traffic). This causes:

  • Empty Road Idling: Green lights stay on for empty roads while cross-streets suffer heavy queues.
  • High Fuel Wastage & Emissions: Vehicles repeatedly idle, increasing carbon emissions by up to 35%.
  • Emergency Delays: Ambulances and fire engines get stuck in red queues without automated clearance.

2. How the AI Model Solves It

This working laptop model integrates a multi-tier intelligent decision pipeline:

  • Perception Layer: Laptop camera or surveillance CCTV detects vehicles using background subtraction and contour classification into cars, heavy vehicles (buses/trucks), and emergency responders.
  • Dynamic Green Time Algorithm: Computes green allocation in real-time proportional to queue density and vehicle passenger weights.
  • Early Cutoff Detection: As soon as the queue clears before the maximum green timer expires, the phase immediately shifts, eliminating wasted seconds.
  • Emergency Vehicle Preemption: Sound sirens and flashing beacons trigger instantaneous green corridor clearing.
